Move your money out, without FVAMPay

This page works on its own, even if FVAMPay is gone. It talks straight to the Base network and sends nothing to FVAMPay or anyone else. You need your account's recovery key: the 12-word recovery phrase you wrote down, or your hardware wallet.

  1. Start a recovery with your recovery key.
  2. Wait 7 days. A passkey on the account can cancel it meanwhile, so nobody can take your money just by finding your words.
  3. Move everything to a wallet of your own, within the next 30 days.

1Network

How this page reaches the network. The network's public one is filled in. Another provider's URL for the same network also works, but this page trusts it for everything it shows you (the balances, the time, whether your key can only send to one wallet), so use one you trust. It can't choose where your money goes: you type that yourself in step 4.

Fill in your account's address above.

    FVAMPay accounts are standard smart accounts on Base (Kernel 0.3.1, ERC-4337 EntryPoint 0.7). Your recovery key may sign only transfers out, one per operation, as typed data that names where the money goes and how much. This page signs one for each thing your account holds and sends it to the EntryPoint from the key's own address: no bundler, no paymaster, no FVAMPay server.
